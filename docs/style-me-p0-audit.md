# Style Me: P0 audit and two-section implementation

Audited 14 October 2026 against every P0 FR and its acceptance criteria in the approved vision PRD, starting from `3002191`. The owner's later screenshot guidance governs the revised interaction: **3.1 Style suggester** and **3.2 Canvas** inside Style Me, sharing the same session. The four supplied Whering screenshots are interaction references, not garment assets or proof of preview quality. No scope reduction to Canvas, freeze or real on-self preview is proposed.

## Experience and phase mapping

| Phase | 3.1 Style suggester | 3.2 Canvas | Shared dependencies / exit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 implementation | Discover-style conversation, Mangalore/Varkala/Himachal/work-trip starters, wardrobe-aware owned/missing suggestions, trip essentials, editable brief and constraints | Free placement of garment cutouts or labelled representatives; move/resize/rotate/stack, pins, clothing layers, undo, searchable Add items tray, local board image, freeze review, Gemini See it on me | All original P0 FRs still apply. Real wardrobe integration, account sync, privacy/recovery and consented preview quality must pass before release. |
| P1 connected workflows | Verified product candidates (e.g. a specific Decathlon jacket), evidence-backed price/stock/size, inspiration recreation, community handoffs | Verified selected Discover candidates, collections/sharing and planning/wear handoffs | My Wardrobe owns ingestion/lifecycle/wear ledger; Discover owns commerce evidence. |
| P2 enrichment | Learning from history, education and optional recaps | Advanced poses/avatars/multiple views and extra creative tools | Only after the P0/P1 journeys are validated. |

The 100–200 ms ambition applies to local manipulation and composition with loaded assets. Pointer movement updates DOM transforms without a network round trip; committed edits are rendered locally before ordered revision-checked saves. A local PNG export is a **collage**, not generated clothing on a body. One browser export of four conceptual pieces took **93 ms** (single observation, not a p95 or device-cohort guarantee). Asset download/decode and large boards can take longer. Gemini on-self generation remains a separate asynchronous P0 capability; no claim of 100–200 ms Gemini rendering is made.

## Quality gates (all still required before P0 release)

- **Snapshot, stale writes, ownership, duplicate mutations, consent and cross-owner access:** automated scenarios pass for the implemented contracts. Canonical wardrobe asset/auth integration adds more required scenarios.
- **100 briefs, ≥85% reviewed wearable results with two reviewers:** not measured. No approved result cohort has been invented.
- **30 consented personal photos × 10 supported combinations, ≥85% recognizability/layering acceptance:** blocked on Gemini access and not measured. The HTTP 429 remains the last live-provider evidence.
- **Cold/warm styling p50/p95, refinement and preview latency; local editing <100 ms p95:** not measured as a cohort. One local PNG export was 93 ms; this does not validate Gemini latency or the general 100–200 ms target.
- **99% valid production-flow reliability over seven days:** not measured; no production deployment made.
- **Strict retention, cross-worker background recovery, real account sync, full accessibility/privacy:** incomplete. Do not release based on fixture coverage alone.

## Canonical wardrobe adapter coordination

Reviewed `origin/claude/my-wardrobe-phase-2` at `0e0d1e5` read-only. Its `cleanItem`/client sync contract includes `id`, name/category/type/colour/tags, `status`, `archived`, `image` and `imageVersion`, but does **not** expose a native item revision or lifecycle event bus. Style Me's `wardrobe-adapter.js` therefore creates a content-derived **projection revision**, not a claim of a My Wardrobe server revision. Available records remain owned; laundry/lent/tailor/stored are unavailable; archive/deletion/sample/unknown-category records are excluded. Indian category mapping uses the record type; ambiguous ethnic records need correction in their owning module.

Host registration after integrating the separately developed module:

```js
window.StyleMe.registerWardrobeAdapter(() => canonicalRecords, {
  schemaVersion: 1,
  canonical: true
});
```

`canonicalRecords` must be the current authorized records, not fixtures, saved product candidates or another account's inventory. Invoke the provider against current host state so corrections/lifecycle changes are visible. The legacy device fallback remains user-described; registration does not turn it into canonical evidence. No branch merge, broad `app.js` rewrite or mutation of Claude's files was performed. Real authorization/lifecycle/account acceptance remains open, including server-side resolution of record IDs and live fidelity evaluation of captured snapshot images.
